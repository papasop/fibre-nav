# Line C development through C155

Evidence snapshot: 23 October 2026. LC1–LC6 are validation targets; C-numbered
identifiers are experimental runs. This page adds a selected development chain
without changing C35–C37's recorded local scope or claiming LC2 closure.

The [evidence package](../evidence/gpt2_lc2_c141_c155/) distinguishes four
original-archive-backed compact exports (C141/C142/C148/C150) from two supplied
execution-log records (C154/C155). Its provenance inventory, actual included
frozen tensors and CPU checker make these tiers explicit. Full predecessor ZIPs
and the two large diagnostic archives remain external.

## Confirmation boundary

C141 freezes the original C140 step12 writer before generating48 declared new
local states in the three represented input classes. In each of two rounds,
raw/C137/C140 pass32/40/42 of48 respectively. C140 retains six response failures;
the smaller-norm subset cannot replace the full denominator. C142 completes a
read-only archive/tensor/arithmetic audit retaining that failure, without a new
model replay or excluded-input evaluation. After exposure, the C141 states are
not eligible as unseen validation data.

## Mechanism development on exposed84-state training data

C148 compares global continuation with fixed three-prototype prewrite context
routing. Both final arms pass79/84 in both rounds. The router is a training-side
mechanism, not established model-internal addressing or a geometric atlas.
Parameter counts36/108 differ, so no isolated routing advantage is established.

C150 reuses the frozen router and adds exactly one registered cyclic coupling.
The diagonal and cyclic final arms pass79/84 and80/84 in both rounds. Four cyclic
response failures remain. Parameters108/216 differ. The final checkpoint and
all failures are retained; neither arm qualifies for new unseen evaluation under
the original all84 training rule. A maximum-error improvement or a pass-count
gain does not replace complete acceptance.

## Numerical diagnostics

The supplied C154 log reports a completed native-forward stencil at early and
late archived positions. At the late position, the gradient dot actual update
is about−3.54e-4 while the positive full-step objective change is about−6.82e-6;
finite-difference slopes vary with scale. Positive updates still reduce the
objective. These finite observations do not certify AD error or a curvature bound.

The supplied C155 log reports all756 FP32 native/functional pairs with exactly
zero observation and KL differences on the sampled points. This finds no
forward-route discrepancy there; it does not independently verify backwards.
FP64 covers only two fixed exposed states and keeps its own and original FP32
response references separate. The two large raw archives were not inspected in
this import, so their statements remain log-tier.

## Status and next capability evidence

LC2 remains open. Current work has not delivered a new fully qualified84-state
writer or a strict excluded-class acceptance result. The next capability route
is to resolve the outstanding training behaviour, freeze the complete writer
and all data-dependent preprocessing/selection, then perform new prospectively
declared confirmation and excluded-input evaluations under unchanged gates.
Exclusion covers fitting, normalisation, data-dependent priors, calibration and
selection. Input classes historically examined by the programme must not be
called historically unseen merely because they are excluded from a new fit.

Public CPU archive checks and fresh GPT-2 model execution are separate evidence
levels. Preparation, fitting, execution, verification and failure costs must not
be conflated. This update changes none of LC3–LC6's open status.
