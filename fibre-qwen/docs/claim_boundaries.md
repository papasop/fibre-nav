# Claim boundaries

1. Training loss is not personalization evidence.
2. Three held-out conversation-derived items are not independent confirmation.
3. R21a/R21b evaluation items were authored for development; R21b reused R21a items.
4. Automated keyword rubrics do not replace human evaluation.
5. Prompt conditioning is not weight learning or continual learning.
6. None of R20–R21 tests the moving-response-fibre optimizer.
7. Failed protocols and reversed examples remain part of the record.
8. R21d's all-zero lexical scores do not establish equality or failure of the three model arms; R21d-r1 localizes a measurement failure only.
9. The R21d-r1 decoding key must remain withheld until blinded judgments are sealed.
