# Claim boundary

v2.4.3 is a post-failure repair evaluated on three newly declared seeds and a
new held-out expression family. **Two of three seeds passed every gate.** It
supports three-seed replication of exact shared-state operations, selectivity,
controls, response/KL eligibility, MOVE/RETURN and the declared L5 criteria
under this configuration. It does not establish a 3/3 unified confirmation,
improved expression generalization relative to v2.4.1, the complete L4
28-relation geometry audit, cross-model transfer, gradient-free memory,
autonomous instruction execution, arbitrary program closure, capacity scaling
or global transport.
