# Claim boundary

## Supported claim

Within the declared finite parameter charts, alpha grids, response budgets and
calibration/held-out splits, the instantaneous response-kernel arm defines a
better held-out low-response learning frontier than the recorded optimizer
update and the declared stale, time-shifted and signed-permuted controls.

The reduced ResNet audit supports this ordering in 7/8 prospective seeds. The
GPT-2 native-LoRA-B audit confirms it within its frozen protocol in 8/8 seeds
and at 24/24 noninitial nodes.

## Not established

- Ordinary AdamW, SGD or large-model training naturally follows the response
  fibre.
- Every response-kernel direction carries adaptive value.
- The two architectures have the same response map, parameter chart or effect
  magnitude.
- The GPT-2 result establishes full-parameter geometry, semantic invariance or
  general language-model behavior.
- A continuous Pareto optimum has been computed beyond the frozen finite
  budget and alpha grids.
- These results establish a global variational law.

Low first-order leakage is partly induced by the kernel construction. The
nontrivial evidence is finite-step held-out utility, calibration-to-held-out
generalization and superiority over all prospectively declared controls.

## Separation from GPTW-v3

The 24/24 noninitial-node count belongs only to the low-response Pareto audit.
The 32/32 interior-node count in GPTW-v3 belongs to a separate
current-versus-source-fixed correction-cost protocol. The counts and statistics
must not be interchanged.
