# Claim boundary

## Supported by one frozen development seed

- A shared category router reached 100% training and 87.5% whole-concept
  held-out accuracy across four declared categories.
- The category-current arm exceeded the shuffled-category and no-graph controls
  on the declared held-out accuracy comparison.
- All 24 evaluated category moves satisfied the declared endpoint eligibility
  constraints.
- Exact and training-expression access passed for every declared concept.

## Not supported

- Confirmed category geometry or category-level generalization.
- Positive margin for every held-out concept.
- Within-category distances being smaller than between-category distances.
- Improvement over the pair-only control.
- Universal held-out linguistic access after category movement.
- Geodesic distance, curvature, parallel transport, a fibre connection or a
  global section.
- Multi-seed or model-family generality.

## Permitted summary

> In one frozen GPT-2 development seed, v1.8.1 partially supports a shared
> category-conditioned writer: training routing was perfect and whole-concept
> held-out routing reached 7/8 while beating shuffled and no-graph controls, but
> the positive-margin, distance-ratio, pair-only and universal held-out-access
> gates did not all pass.
