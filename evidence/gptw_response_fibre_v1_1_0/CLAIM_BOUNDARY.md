- full-parameter GPT-2 response-fibre behavior;
- semantic invariance, broad natural-language-task generality or downstream task improvement;
- ordinary SGD, Adam or unconstrained training following the constructed paths;
- complete response-kernel transport or a global smooth fibre bundle;

- Euler-Lagrange stationarity, arbitrary-path minimality or a global variational law.

V3 does not test natural SGD/Adam trajectories.
