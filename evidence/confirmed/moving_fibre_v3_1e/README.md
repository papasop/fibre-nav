# Moving-fibre realizability-cost zero-slope confirmation v3.1e

v3.1e is a prospective methodological repair of the single invalid gate in
v3.1d. It uses 16 entirely new seeds (70726--70741) and leaves the model,
online causal construction, four Fisher radii, common 20% endpoint, response
retraction, admissibility rules, cost ratios, and exponent-separation gates
unchanged.

The v3.1d fixed-source arm correctly approached a nonzero constant, but a
high-R-squared requirement is ill-conditioned when the total variance of a
constant control is nearly zero. v3.1e therefore freezes asymmetric tests that
match the two distinct predictions:

- moving arm: exponent at least 0.50 and log--log R-squared at least 0.80;
- fixed arm: absolute exponent at most 0.25 and four-point relative span at
  most 0.20;
- moving-minus-fixed exponent gap at least 0.25.

The complete confirmation still requires at least 14 comparable seeds and at
least 12 seeds passing the substantive scaling, flatness, ratio, depth,
quadrature, and response-row-space gates. Fixed-arm R-squared remains in the
output as a diagnostic but cannot veto a theoretically constant sequence.

This is a restricted TinyCNN/MNIST moving-fibre realizability-cost test, not an
exact continuum theorem, arbitrary-path global variation, GPT-2 transfer, or
universal intelligence law. Run `COLAB_LAUNCHER_REALISABILITY_V3_1E.py` and
upload this ZIP. An A100 is strongly recommended.
